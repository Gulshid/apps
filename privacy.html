Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026. This is the same policy shown inside the FitTrack app.

FitTrack is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using FitTrack you agree to the practices described here.

01 Information we collect

Account information

Name and email from your Google account when you sign in, your username and display name (friends can see these), an optional profile photo, and profile details such as fitness goal, diet type, height, weight, and units.

Health and fitness data you enter

Food, meal and recipe diary entries; water; sleep; workouts; weight; body measurements; progress photos; fasting; supplements and medication; grocery lists; and meal plans.

Health Connect

Only if you turn on health sync: steps, active calories and sleep are read; weight and workouts are written back.

Friends and challenges

Friend requests, challenges you join, and your leaderboard rank.

AI features

Coach messages, food descriptions, food photos, and the data they reference, sent to Google Gemini to produce a reply. Coach conversations are stored in your account.

Device and usage data

Reminders you schedule, feature-usage data, feedback and ratings you submit, app settings, time zone, and Firebase App Check signals.

02 How we use your information

We use your information to provide and improve FitTrack. We never use your health data for advertising, and we do not build advertising profiles. We use it to provide logging, summaries, progress charts, reminders, challenges and the AI coach; to sign you in and keep data in sync; to send the reminders you schedule; to generate weekly summaries and calorie adjustments; to answer support requests; and to keep the app secure and fix bugs.

03 Friends and sharing

Friends see your username, display name, and challenge and leaderboard results. Share cards send an image you choose through your device's share sheet. We do not sell your data.

04 Health data

Health and fitness information is sensitive. We do not sell it, do not use it for advertising or data mining, and do not share it with data brokers. We use health data only to provide the features you turn on. You can stop health sync and revoke permissions at any time in FitTrack or Health Connect.

05 Third-party services

Google Firebase (sign-in, data storage and app-integrity checks); Google Sign-In; Google Gemini API (AI coach replies, food descriptions and scans, summaries and calorie adjustments); Cloudinary (profile photos, scanned food photos and progress photos); food and product databases (Nutritionix, USDA FoodData Central, Open Food Facts, TheMealDB and RapidAPI product lookups), which receive the food name or barcode you search for; and Health Connect, which syncs on your device only when you turn it on.

06 Storage and security

Your data is stored in Google Firebase, which encrypts data in transit and at rest. Some data is kept on your device so the app works offline; offline changes sync when you reconnect. Backup files you create are saved where you choose, and you are responsible for keeping them safe. Exports you create are shared through your device's share sheet.

07 Permissions

Camera: food and barcode scanning, and progress photos. Photos and files: uploading photos and selecting restore files. Microphone: voice input for food logging. Notifications and exact alarms: reminders you schedule. Motion and activity: step counting on Android. Health Connect: health sync you turn on.

08 Data retention and deletion

You can delete your account at any time in Settings → Delete Account. This removes your sign-in account and profile, your diary, body, supplement, medication, reminder and friend data, and your summaries, achievements and coach conversations. Account data is deleted within 30 days of your request. Images stored with Cloudinary are not removed automatically; email us to have them removed.

09 Children

FitTrack is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.
