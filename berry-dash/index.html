How to play

One thumb is enough

Every move is a tap or a swipe. Learn the four moves, then time your jumps and slides to dodge hedges, blocks and bars.

↑

Jump

Tap the screen or swipe up to jump over obstacles.

↓

Slide

Swipe down to slide under overhead obstacles.

←→

Switch lanes

Swipe left or right to move between lanes.

+

Collect

Grab berries and gems along the way, then spend your coins in the shop.

Features

More than one run

Come back each day for rewards, chase achievements and compete with players around the world.

Power-ups

Pick up power-ups mid-run to help you go further.

  • Magnet
  • Shield
  • Double berries
  • Slow-mo

Daily rewards

A 7-day reward calendar. Come back each day to claim the next reward.

Achievements and missions

Earn trophies for milestones such as First Steps, Berry Picker and Speed Demon. Complete missions to earn coins.

Global leaderboard

Pick a display name and see where your best score ranks against other players.

Seasonal events

Limited-time events bring bonus rewards, such as a 1.5x reward multiplier.

Revive once per run

After a hit, continue your run once by spending coins or watching a short rewarded ad. Watching is always your choice.

Privacy

Your progress stays on your device

  • Your progress, coins and settings are saved on your device.
  • The game uses an anonymous account. Sign in with Google only if you want to link it.
  • Your display name and best score appear on the public leaderboard.
  • Rewarded ads come from Google AdMob. You choose whether to watch them.
