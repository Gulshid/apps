Everything you need in one calorie tracker

Start with the food diary and add the rest as you need them. Each feature works on its own, and most of them feed your weekly summary.

Food diary

Log meals with a search of food databases, a barcode scan or a food photo. Save the foods and recipes you eat often.

AI nutrition coach

Ask about your day, describe a meal, or get a suggestion for dinner. Replies use your own logs and goals.

Recipes and meal plans

Build recipes, plan seven days of meals, and turn the plan into a grocery list.

Water, sleep and workouts

Track water intake, log sleep and record exercise. Each one shows its own trend over time.

Weight and body measurements

Record your weight, body measurements and progress photos, then watch the trend rather than the daily number.

Fasting timer

Run intermittent fasting windows with a timer and alerts that match your schedule.

Supplements and reminders

Keep a list of supplements and medication, and set your own reminders for anything you need to remember.

Friend challenges

Add friends, join a challenge and see where you rank on the leaderboard. Friends see only your username and challenge results.

Streaks and achievements

Earn badges for consistent logging and see a weekly summary of what changed.
