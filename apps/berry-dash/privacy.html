Berry Dash

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026. This policy covers the Berry Dash game for Android (package com.gulshid.berry).

Overview

Berry Dash is a game. It stores your progress on your device, lets you appear on a public leaderboard, and shows optional rewarded ads. This policy explains what data is involved and what you can do about it.

Information the game handles

Anonymous account

When you open the game, Firebase Authentication creates an anonymous account for you. It gives you a user ID that identifies your progress and leaderboard entry. You do not have to give a name, email address or password to play.

Google sign-in (optional)

You can link your anonymous account to a Google account. Firebase Authentication then keeps the account details that Google provides for that sign-in. The game does not store your email address in its own data.

Leaderboard (public)

If you submit a score, your user ID, the display name you choose, your best score, your coin total and the time of your last update are stored in a leaderboard that other players can see.

On your device

Your progress, coins, unlocked characters, achievements, missions and settings (sound, music and haptics) are saved on your device so the game can continue where you left off.

Ads

Rewarded ads are provided by Google AdMob. You can choose to watch one, for example to continue a run. AdMob may collect device identifiers and ad interaction data as described in Google's privacy policy.

Analytics

The game does not send usage analytics to a service in its release version.

How we use this information

We use it to run the game, keep your progress, show the leaderboard, check submitted scores against plausible limits, and offer rewarded ads that you choose to watch.

Third-party services

Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore, which stores the leaderboard), Google Sign-In (only if you choose to link a Google account), and Google AdMob (rewarded ads).

How the leaderboard is protected

Firestore security rules allow each player to write only their own leaderboard entry, and they reject entries that are malformed or outside the allowed score and name limits.

Permissions

The game asks for internet access and network status so it can load the leaderboard, sign-in and ads.

Your choices and deleting data

You can reset your progress on your device from the game's Settings. To remove your leaderboard entry or your linked account data, email gulshidzada4@gmail.com from the address you used with the game, or include your display name, so we can find your entry.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version. For questions, email gulshidzada4@gmail.com.
