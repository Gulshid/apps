Type it naturally. Get it done.

Features

Everything you need to plan a day

Smart Todo keeps the input simple, so you spend your time on the task rather than the form.

Natural language entry

Type “buy milk tomorrow 9am” or “urgent: send report”. Dates, times, priority and categories are read from the text.

Reminders and alarms

Get notified at the time you set. Reminders are rescheduled after your phone restarts.

Voice input

Tap the microphone and say your task. It is added to your list the same way typed text is.

Focus mode

Work through one task with a timer. Start a focus session and see how much time you spent.

Insights

Look back on your progress, streaks and focus time in the Insights screen.

Points and badges

Earn points for each task you complete, and unlock badges for streaks and milestones.

Home screen widget

See today's tasks on your home screen, and add a new one from the quick settings tile.

Backup and restore

Export your tasks to a file and import them back on another phone, whenever you choose.

How it works

Three steps, every day

01

Type or speak

Write or say what needs doing, with the day and time if you know them.

02

Get reminded

The task is saved with its date, time and priority, and you get a notification on time.

03

Finish and review

Mark tasks done, earn points, and look back on your progress in Insights.

Privacy

Your tasks stay on your phone

  • Tasks are stored on your device. There is no account and no sign-in.
  • The app has no ads and no analytics.
  • The microphone is used only when you tap it for voice input.
