Smart Todo Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Smart Todo is developed by Gulshid Zada. This policy explains what information the app uses and how. In short: your tasks stay on your device, there is no account, and the app has no ads and no analytics.

1. Information stored on your device

Smart Todo saves your tasks, categories, reminders, focus sessions, points, badges and settings in local storage on your phone. This data is not uploaded to our servers, because the app has no servers that receive it. Uninstalling the app deletes this local data.

2. Accounts

Smart Todo does not ask you to create an account or sign in.

3. Voice input

When you tap the microphone, the app uses your phone's speech recognition to turn your voice into text. The app only listens when you tap the microphone. Depending on your phone and its settings, speech recognition may be processed by your phone manufacturer's or Google's speech service. Please check your device's speech recognition settings for details.

4. Notifications and alarms

Reminders are scheduled on your device. After a restart, the app reschedules them using the Android boot receiver. No notification data is sent to us.

5. Backup and sharing

You can export your tasks to a file and share it through the Android share sheet, for example to your own cloud storage or another app. The file is created and sent only when you choose to do so. Importing a backup reads a file you select. Once a file leaves the app, the app no longer controls it, and the destination app's policy applies.

7. Advertising, analytics and tracking

Smart Todo contains no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs. We do not track you across apps or websites.

8. Children

Smart Todo does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect personal information at all.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
