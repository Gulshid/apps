Features

Everything you need in one calorie tracker

Start with the food diary and add the rest as you need it. Most features feed your weekly summary, so every log adds up.

Food diary that logs in seconds

Search food databases, scan a barcode or snap a photo. Save the foods and recipes you eat most, and see calories, protein, carbs and fat for each meal.

  • Search, barcode and photo logging
  • Saved foods and recipes for repeat meals
  • Daily totals against your calorie goal

AI nutrition coach

Ask about your day, describe a meal or get ideas for dinner. Replies use your own logs and goals.

Recipes and meal plans

Build recipes, plan seven days of meals, and turn the plan into a grocery list.

Water, sleep and workouts

Track water toward your daily goal, log sleep and record exercise. Each one shows its own trend.

Weight and progress

Record weight, body measurements and progress photos. Watch the trend rather than the daily number.

Fasting timer

Run intermittent fasting windows with a timer and alerts that match your schedule.

Reminders and supplements

Keep a list of supplements and medication, and set your own reminders for anything you need to remember.

Friend challenges

Join a challenge and see where you rank. Friends see only your username and challenge results.

Streaks and weekly summary

Earn badges for consistent logging and read a weekly summary of what changed.

How it works

Log, learn, adjust

The whole app follows one loop. Logs give the coach and summaries something to work with, and the results show you what to change.

01

Log your day

Meals, water, sleep, workouts and weight, from one diary.

02

Get guidance

The coach reads your logs and suggests the next small change.

03

Review the trend

Charts and weekly summaries show what moved and what did not.

Privacy

Your health data stays yours

Health and fitness information is sensitive, so we keep the data we use limited to what the app needs.

Never sold

We do not sell your data. We never use your health data for advertising or build advertising profiles.

You choose what to share

Health sync stays off until you turn it on. Friends see only your username, display name and challenge results.

Delete it any time

Delete your account in Settings. Account data is removed within 30 days of your request.
